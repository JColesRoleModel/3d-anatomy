Alertness, High Energy & Awake ⚡

Need a quick lift? These gaze flows deliver sympathetic drive, dopamine lift and razor‑sharp focus.

Ignition Sequence 🚀

  • Upward vertical gaze hold (30–45 sec)
  • Rapid left–right saccades (20–30 sec)
  • Upper‑right diagonal hold (20–30 sec)
  • Circle‑wide clockwise (1 slow rotation)
  • Center point reset (10 sec)

Dopamine Spark ✨

  • Straight up hold
  • Straight right hold
  • Micro pulses up–right–up–right (10 rounds)
  • Cross‑midline fast tracking (20 sec)

Immediate Reboot ⚡

  • Fast horizontal saccades (15 sec)
  • Upward hold (20 sec)
  • Clockwise circles (1 rotation)
  • Done—instant lift!

Grounding, Balance & Harmony 🌿

Enter a relaxed, coherent state—parasympathetic dominance without sedation. These flows cultivate vagal tone and hemispheric balance.

The Equilibrium Pattern 🌊

  • Downward diagonal left hold (20–30 sec)
  • Slow horizontal tracking left→right→left (2 passes)
  • Soft center gaze + 4‑second nasal breath (20 sec)
  • Downward vertical hold (20–30 sec)
  • Counter‑clockwise slow circle (1 slow rotation)

The Floating Bridge 🌈

  • Upper‑left diagonal hold
  • Slow‑focus expansion (soften eyes outward)
  • Downward hold
  • Horizontal smooth tracking

Neutral Centering 🧘‍♂️

  • Downward gaze hold (30 sec)
  • Center gaze softening (20 sec)
  • Slow horizontal tracking (2 passes)

Restoring & Letting Go 🌑

For deep restoration, limbic unloading and dissolving patterned tension. These sequences invite surrender and integration.

The Release Pattern 💧

  • Downward‑left hold (20–40 sec)
  • Downward‑right hold (20–40 sec)
  • Slow counter‑clockwise circles (1–2 rotations)
  • Soft eyes / almost‑closed gaze (20 sec)
  • Center drop + exhale focus (3 breaths)

Limbic Dissolve 🧠✨

  • Downward vertical hold
  • Micro‑saccade down (gentle tapping movements)
  • Upper‑left slow tracking
  • Softened peripheral vision (20 sec)

Letting‑Go Trinity 🌬️

  • Down‑left hold (30 sec)
  • Down‑right hold (30 sec)
  • Soft center gaze (20 sec)
