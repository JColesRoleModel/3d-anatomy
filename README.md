# Tree of Life: Neurological Research Institute

An interactive 3D journey through the human body and its occult anatomy, set in a living night sky. Filter by body system, tap any structure to see its name, and install it on your phone as an app.

**Open it:** https://jcolesrolemodel.github.io/3d-anatomy/

## Add it to your phone
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

Each system downloads the first time you turn it on and then works offline.

## The sky and the art
`cosmos.js` builds the stage around the body. The night sky (aurora and nebula after the two album covers Brian shared, a milky way and about 60,000 twinkling stars) breathes on a nine-second cycle. Shooting stars cross it every few seconds, and every 16 to 42 seconds a random event happens: a meteor shower, a lightning storm that lights up the pyramid, a comet, a nova or an aurora surge.

Around the body stand the pieces of the *Secret of the Ages* poster in 3D: the rose and blue Ionic pillars with the violet middle pillar, the emerald cross, the golden Tree of Life, the crown of rays, the star of David as a star tetrahedron, a zodiac wheel with the planets in their home signs, an orbiting zodiac ring, the Aquarian jar pouring light, the bag of magic dust, the pyramid with its shining capstone, and Saturn with its hexagon storm. The emerald tablet and the caduceus drift through the background. Everything is drawn in code, none of it can be tapped, and the pieces near the body fade when you zoom in. The ✦ button turns the sky, the art or the motion off, and can call up a sky event; motion starts off for people who ask their device to reduce motion.

The occult symbols breathe with the sky and light flows up the nadis.

The skin layer is smoothed like a mannequin at the groin (`tools/modesty.mjs`); the organs layer keeps the anatomical genitals.

## Systems and layers
Skeletal, muscular, fascia, circulatory, lymphatic, nervous, endocrine, sinuses, organs, skin, and joints and ligaments. A structure can belong to more than one system (the thymus, for example, is lymphatic and endocrine).

Each system has its own opacity slider and sub-filters, for example:
- **Skeletal:** skull, teeth, cervical, thoracic and lumbar spine, sacrum and coccyx, ribs, arms, pelvis, legs
- **Muscular:** depth (superficial, intermediate, deep) and body region
- **Nervous:** brain, spinal cord, cranial nerves (each nerve I–XII separately), spinal nerves, eye and tear ducts, ear
- **Endocrine:** each gland

Tap a part, then tap any group in its info card (like *Cranial nerves* or *Lumbar vertebrae*) to focus on the whole group while everything else fades.

Muscle depth isn't labelled in the source models. `tools/muscle-depth.mjs` measures it by checking, for points all over each muscle, how many other muscles sit between that point and the skin. Textbook groupings from the source (such as "Deep part of posterior compartment of leg") take priority, and `tools/catalog.mjs` has a short list of manual corrections.

## Occult anatomy
Seven overlay layers built from Brian's Canva boards (OCCULT ANATOMY OF MAN whiteboard, UNIVERSAL MAP, 7 CHAKRAS): chakras, elements and prana vayus, the pineal / pituitary / thalamus trinity, the hand map and mudras, the Tree of Life, nadis and kundalini, and electric / magnetic polarity. Tap any symbol for its card. Values the boards mark as unsure (?, ???), leave blank, or contradict each other on show in amber with an asterisk and a note.

The polarity layer also lists what Franz Bardon's *Initiation into Hermetics* says about man, woman and handedness, with the two places it differs from the board (the inside of the hands, and the feet, which the board leaves out).

The layer data lives in `data/occult.json`, generated by `node tools/occult.mjs` (it reads the models to place each symbol on the body). Edit the tables in that script, not the JSON.

## Path tracing

The info card for an artery, vein or nerve can trace it back to the heart, brainstem or spinal cord, out to all its branches, or along the route to a second part (`trace.js`). The branching lives in `data/paths.json`, generated by `node tools/paths.mjs`. Most links come from the nesting in the original Z-Anatomy files (which `build.mjs` flattens away, so the script reads the raw files); the heart, the brachial, lumbar and sacral plexuses, the brainstem nuclei, the sinuses and a few corrections are written by hand in that script. Every link is checked against the 3D models, and joins farther apart than 4 mm are drawn as dotted lines. The model has no separate lateral and medial cords, lumbar plexus or sacral plexus, so those are stand-in points.

## Conditions: scapular winging

Layers > Conditions opens a study card for scapular winging (`conditions.js`, text and sources in `data/conditions.json`). Each type (medial, lateral, rhomboid, secondary, pseudo, plus normal for comparison) shows the weak muscle, its nerve traced back to the spinal cord, numbered spots where that nerve is commonly injured, and effects with evidence badges. The arm can be raised forward or to the side, with a slider for how weak the muscle is.

The movement is illustrative. The collarbone, shoulder blade and arm move as rigid bones on a normal shoulder rhythm (Ludewig et al.), changed by made-up but plausible amounts for each type; everything else around the shoulder is bent to follow them by blending between the nearest bones. Only the right side moves.

## How it's built
- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with [three.js](https://threejs.org) (bundled in `vendor/`). No build step: GitHub Pages serves the files as they are.
- `models/*.glb`: one model per source file, simplified and compressed for phones.
- `data/catalog.json`: every structure with its system tags and anatomical grouping. This is where study notes and extra layers will go.
- `tools/`: the scripts that converted the source models (see below).

### Rebuilding the models
The source models are the Z-Anatomy FBX files from https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy (`Resources/Models/FBX`).
```
npm install   # the tools' packages, pinned in package.json
node_modules/fbx2gltf/bin/<OS>/FBX2glTF --binary --input SkeletalSystem100.fbx --output raw/skeletal
node tools/build.mjs raw/skeletal.glb out/skeletal.glb skeletal 0.3   # also writes out/skeletal.json; copy it to tools/meta/
node tools/muscle-depth.mjs out/regions.glb out/muscular.glb data/catalog.json tools/meta/muscle-depth.json
node tools/modesty.mjs        # once the regions model is in models/: smooths the groin of the skin layer
node tools/catalog.mjs data/catalog.json
```
`tools/meta/` holds the structure lists that `build.mjs` writes next to each model.

### Accounts
Saved views sync to a person's account through Firebase (project `tree-of-life-nri`). `firebase-config.js` holds the web app config, `firestore.rules` the security rules (paste them into the Firebase console under Firestore > Rules), and `vendor/firebase.js` the SDK, bundled by `node tools/firebase-bundle.mjs`.
