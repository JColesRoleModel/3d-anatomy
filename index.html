Tree of Life:Neurological Research Institute

Tap a system to show or hide it. Open Layers (or the ▾ next to a system) for sub-filters like spine regions, muscle depth and cranial nerves, plus an opacity slider and colour for each system. Tap the small circle next to any sub-filter, or the colour swatch in a part's info card, to give it its own colour. In a part's info card, tap any group name, such as Cranial nerves, to focus on the whole group while everything else fades. Drag to rotate, pinch or scroll to zoom, and two-finger drag to pan. Tap any structure to see its name. On a computer, press ? for keyboard controls.

The Occult anatomy layers at the bottom of the Layers panel show the chakras, elements and prana vayus, the pineal–pituitary–thalamus trinity, the hand map and mudras, the Tree of Life, the nadis and electric / magnetic polarity. Tap a symbol on the body, or a name in the panel, to read it. Values marked * are incomplete, unsure or conflicting on the source boards.

The night sky breathes on a slow nine-second cycle. Shooting stars cross it every few seconds, and every half minute or so something rarer happens: a meteor shower, a lightning storm, a comet, a nova or an aurora surge. Around the body stand the pieces of the Secret of the Ages poster in 3D: the pillars, the emerald cross, the Tree of Life, the crown of rays, the star of David as a turning star tetrahedron, the zodiac wheel and ring, the Aquarian jar, the bag of magic dust, the pyramid, and Saturn with its hexagon storm. The emerald tablet and the caduceus drift through the background. The art fades away when you zoom in to study. Tap ✦ to turn the sky, the art or the motion off.
